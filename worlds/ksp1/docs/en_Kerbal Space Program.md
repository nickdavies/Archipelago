# Kerbal Space Program

## Where is the settings page?

The [player settings page for this game](../player-settings) contains all the options for configuring your randomizer
experience.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Rocket parts are shuffled into the multiworld item pool. You start with a minimal set of parts and must receive more
from the pool to unlock new mission capabilities. Mission completions (orbits, landings, returns) are the location
checks.

## What is the goal?

Complete the configured goal mission, such as a crewed Mun landing or visiting all bodies in the Jool system.
